This Privacy Policy explains how DigiCam: Retro Film Camera ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

In short: the App works entirely offline. Your photos are taken, processed and saved on your device only. We do not run servers, we do not collect personal data, and nothing you shoot ever leaves your phone unless you choose to share it yourself.

1. Information the App processes

  • Camera. The App uses your device camera to show a live viewfinder and to take photos. Retro film effects (colour, grain, vignette, light leaks, date stamp, instant-print borders) are applied on your device.
  • Saved photos. Finished photos are saved to your device gallery in Pictures/DigiCam. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party. You can delete them at any time from your gallery.
  • Date stamp. When the date stamp is on, the App prints your device's current date onto the photo. The date is read locally and is not stored or sent anywhere else.

2. What we do NOT do

  • We do not require an account or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not upload, store or view your photos on any server.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, device identifiers or browsing history.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share personal data — we do not have any.

3. Permissions

  • Camera — to show the viewfinder and capture photos.
  • Storage (Android 9 and below only) — to save photos to your gallery. Newer Android versions do not need this permission.

The App does not request internet access.

4. Third-party services

The App does not integrate any third-party services that receive your data. If you open a photo in another app (for example your gallery or a share target), that app's own privacy policy applies.

5. Children

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Data retention & deletion

Photos stay on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the App removes its settings; photos already saved to your gallery remain until you delete them yourself.

7. Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data differently (for example downloadable film packs or in-app purchases), we will update this policy before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.

8. Contact

1. Thông tin Ứng dụng xử lý

  • Camera. Ứng dụng dùng camera để hiển thị khung ngắm và chụp ảnh. Các hiệu ứng phim retro (màu, hạt grain, vignette, light leak, dấu ngày, viền ảnh lấy liền) được áp dụng ngay trên thiết bị.
  • Ảnh đã lưu. Ảnh hoàn chỉnh được lưu vào thư viện ảnh trong Pictures/DigiCam. Ảnh không bao giờ được tải lên máy chủ của chúng tôi hay bên thứ ba nào. Bạn có thể xoá ảnh bất kỳ lúc nào trong thư viện.
  • Dấu ngày. Khi bật dấu ngày, Ứng dụng in ngày hiện tại của thiết bị lên ảnh. Ngày được đọc cục bộ, không lưu trữ hay gửi đi đâu khác.

  • Không tải lên, lưu trữ hay xem ảnh của bạn trên bất kỳ máy chủ nào.
